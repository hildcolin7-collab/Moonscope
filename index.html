How the Signal Score works

Every coin gets a score from 0 to 100, rebuilt each time prices refresh. Higher means more is happening right now.

Volume spike · 25 pts

Last hour's volume compared with its average hour over the past day. Four times normal earns full points.

Buy pressure · 20 pts

Share of buys versus sells in the last hour. 75% buys or more earns full points.

Trade activity · 15 pts

Trades in the last hour against the daily average.

Momentum · 15 pts

Price change over 1 hour and 6 hours.

Liquidity depth · 15 pts

Pool size as a share of market cap. Deeper pools mean less slippage and are harder to drain.

Community · 10 pts

Listed website and socials, plus paid boosts on DexScreener.

Crash brake

A coin down more than 80% in 24 hours or with under $5K liquidity has its score cut in half.

Safety check

Separate from the score. It flags thin liquidity, crashes, brand-new pairs, missing socials and heavy selling. For Solana coins, the RugCheck button runs an on-chain scan of holders and token authorities.
